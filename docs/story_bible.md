## Logline
> 🟡 Name update only (hero renamed): awaiting Ross's OK

Ten years ago, scrap courier Tally Kincaid's mom roared off into the starless Quiet to answer a distress call nobody else had the guts to touch. Now the Hegemony wants that call shut up for good, so Tally is going to answer it herself, and if an entire empire wants to stand in her way, that's honestly the empire's problem.

## World & history
> 🟡 Name update only (hero renamed): awaiting Ross's OK

**The Outer Marches.** The ragged edge of the galaxy, where the stars thin out and the people get tougher. Dust moons, ring stations, rowdy trading ports. Everybody's broke, everything's held together with tape, and anybody will lend you a wrench. Harrow, Tally's home, is one dust moon out of hundreds, and by local accounts the loudest.

**Jump lanes.** Invisible highways that cross days of space in hours. Flying off-lane is slow and a terrible idea. Pilots hand down lane charts like secret family chili recipes. Whoever holds the lanes holds the Marches. The last lane ends at the Rim.

**The Hegemony.** The empire running the Marches from the Yards, a shipyard world wrapped in docks that never stop building. It brings roads, order, taxes and a truly heroic amount of paperwork, and backs it all with a navy. Its admirals give speeches. Its grunts mostly want lunch.

**The Quiet and the call.** Past the Rim lies the Quiet: a starless dark where compasses spin and radios hiss. Ships go in; none have come out. Yet. Out of it comes the call, a distress signal any cheap receiver can catch, asking for help in plain words, nonstop, forever. Old spacers swear it sounded different when they were young; everyone else rolls their eyes. The Hegemony made answering it a crime "for safety," which instantly made it the coolest crime on the frontier.

**The lamp in the window.** Families with someone out in the Quiet light a lamp in the window every night until that someone comes home. Nobody's ever gotten to blow one out. Tally plans to be first.

**Where fantasy lives.** Sci-fi is the hardware; fantasy is the culture. Spacers wear lucky charms, belt out lane-songs through every jump, swear by deck-side fortune readers, and trade Quiet ghost stories at the bar. The relay masts on the dust moons, older than any records, are half ruin, half shrine. Everyone is a chibi animal and nobody ever mentions it: species is never a joke, a trait, a power or a plot point.

**What the public knows (timeline)**
- **Before records:** The lanes, the relay masts, the call and the lamp custom are already there. Nobody knows who started any of it.
- **About 200 years ago:** The Hegemony rolls out from the Yards and plants its flag on the Marches.
- **About 30 years ago:** Salvage crews start hauling derelicts off the edge of the Quiet: hulls of every make and age, some no shipyard recognizes. A rising officer named Sarrow Vane bolts the modern navy together out of them and paints the whole mess gray.
- **About 25 years ago:** The Answering Ban. Crossing the Rim to answer the call becomes a crime.
- **10 years ago:** Tally's mom, Harrow's hottest pilot, defies the ban and blasts off into the Quiet. Harrow still tells the story, and it gets bigger every time.
- **Now:** Hegemony crews start bolting jammers onto the old relay masts, starting with Harrow's. Big mistake.

In the vertical slice, the Signals Corps is jamming Harrow's mast, the Tenders are bell-ringing it into a headache, most Marchfolk are placing bets on who cracks first, and the Coldrunners are selling snacks to both sides. Tally is right in the middle: a courier who runs the odd Coldrunner crate, lights a lamp the Tenders would bless, and is already picking a fight with Kasp. In Act 1 the Navy locks Harrow down with maximum fanfare, which knocks the Marchfolk off the fence, leaves the Coldrunners deciding whether Tally is worth more as a bounty or a passenger, and cracks the Signals Corps wide open when Vela stops jamming the call and starts listening to it.

## Main cast
> 🟡 DRAFT: awaiting Ross's approval (Mox and Ruo already approved)

Tally, Otis and Mox are the vertical slice party. Vela joins at the end of Act 1; Ruo joins in Act 2. Tally never speaks, so the other four carry every conversation, and each one "translates" her differently: Mox narrates what she's obviously thinking (always wrong, always heroic), Otis reads her right with one glance, Vela logs her nods as official statements, and Ruo scores her silences for style.

### Tally Kincaid
- **Role:** Hero, silent protagonist, and the party's quick front-line fighter. A scrap courier on Harrow who has lit her mom's window lamp every single night for ten years. Playable from the first second of the game (slice party). She never speaks a line of dialogue: the party and NPCs do the talking, and Tally answers with her face, her ears, her fists and her feet. Default name **Tally Kincaid**; the player can rename her at the start. Gender: female (she/her), which keeps the approved Logline, World and Factions text as written.
- **Look:** Chibi dog, a scrappy mutt pup with tawny-red fur, a cream muzzle and chest, and a short tail that wags whether she wants it to or not. Her ears are her trademark: one stands straight up, one flops over, and the up-ear perks even higher when she's fired up. Wears her mom's beat-up red flight jacket, three sizes too big, sleeves rolled into fat cuffs, with Harrow patches and a hand-painted lamp on the back. Goggles pushed up between the ears, courier satchel, scuffed boots. Signature item: a heavy brass hand lamp on a short chain clipped to her belt, which she swings like a flail. Silhouette: one ear up, giant jacket, little lamp swinging at her hip.
- **Personality:** Stubborn hope at full volume, without a single word: she would rather be wrong and moving than right and waiting. She's all big grins, bigger swings and zero hesitation, and she is the most loyal person on the frontier. If somebody asks for help, she has already started running.
- **Battle style:** Fast hits and lamp-swing combos, great at starting a Relay chain; signature move **Porch Light**, a blinding lamp flare followed by a full-spin wallop.
- **Arc:** Tally sets out answering one call for one person, and over the journey ends up fighting for every lamp-lit window in the Marches. She learns that hope isn't just refusing to quit; it's letting people come along with you. Because she can't say it, the staging says it: early cutscenes frame her alone, out in front; by the end, the whole crew is in the shot beside her. (twist-related beats: see RESTRICTED section)
- **How they show it:**
  - *The lamp check.* Every night at the window (also her rest/save animation), she lights the brass lamp, taps the glass twice with a knuckle, and gives the dark sky a big thumbs-up, like somebody out there can see it.
  - *Already running.* Whenever anyone asks for help, she doesn't nod and she doesn't wait: before they finish the sentence, she snaps her goggles down and the camera cuts to her already halfway out the door. The NPC finishes the sentence to an empty room.
  - *Legend correction.* When a barfly tells a wildly exaggerated tale of her exploits ("she beat fifty troopers with one paw!"), her up-ear twitches, and she holds up three fingers to set the record straight... then thinks about it, holds up two more, and flexes.
- **Alternates for Ross:** **Cat:** a scrappy tuxedo cat with white socks, a crooked tail tip and the same giant red jacket. · **Tiger cub:** a round orange tiger cub with bold black stripes, chunky paws and little round ears, jacket and goggles unchanged.

### Otis Kettle
- **Role:** Tank, and later the crew's walker pilot. Harrow's dock boss and the closest thing the docks have to a dad. Joins in the slice when Sergeant Kasp's crew starts shoving his dockhands around (slice party). At the end of Act 1, Tally "borrows" his cargo walker, Biscuit, to bust off Harrow, and Otis comes along to make sure nobody scratches the paint.
- **Look:** Chibi brown bear, big and round, with a grizzled gray muzzle and small round ears poking through two holes cut in his hard hat. Faded orange dockworker coveralls, a padded work vest, the hard hat with a little headlamp, thick work gloves, and a dented tin of cough drops in his chest pocket. Signature item: a dented cargo-container door with a handle welded on, used as a shield. Silhouette: a friendly boulder carrying a door.
- **Personality:** The gentlest soul on Harrow: he hums old work songs, calls everybody "friend," and never, ever raises his voice, even while lifting a trooper over his head. Quirk: he politely apologizes to every enemy before he flattens them, then tucks a cough drop in the pocket of anyone he knocks out. Every night he counts his dockhands off shift by name and won't lock up until the count comes out right. He used to pilot for the Navy and walked off one day without explanation; at heart he believes every crew deserves someone waiting for them on the dock. He's the one person who reads Tally perfectly, and usually the one telling Mox to sit down.
- **Battle style:** Soaks hits, shields allies to protect Relay chains from breaking; signature move **Heave-Ho**, picking up one enemy and setting it down, very firmly, on another.
- **Arc:** Otis has spent years keeping his head down and his dock quiet, and the journey asks him to stand up and get loud for something. He's the kettle that never whistles, until the one time he raises his voice in the whole game, and it should rattle the speakers. (twist-related beats: see RESTRICTED section)
- **Sample lines:**
  - *"Sorry about this, friend. Nothing personal. Mostly structural."*
  - *"A dock is just a long wait with a roof on it. Somebody has to stand at the end of it."*
  - *"No, son, that look does not mean 'unleash the prototype.' That look means 'put the prototype down.'"*

### Mox
> ✅ APPROVED by Ross, 2026-10-05

- **Role:** Mechanic and gadget fighter; the crew's engineer. A Harrow dock kid who joins in the slice by stowing away in Tally's delivery crate on the run to the Old Relay Tower (slice party). Tally tries to send him home several times. It never works.
- **Look:** Chibi ferret kid, long and wiry, with a welding mask flipped up on his head like a crown. An oversized utility vest with far too many pockets, each one bulging with something, plus a wrench nearly as tall as he is. A small, wobbly homemade drone named Tuesday (built on a Tuesday) buzzes over his shoulder. Every gadget he builds is welded with "MOX WUZ HERE." Silhouette: mask, wrench, pockets, drone.
- **Personality:** Brags nonstop about inventions that are about forty percent finished. Quirk: he panics exactly once per crisis, loudly, for about four seconds, then gets to work, so the crew has learned to just wait it out. Underneath the boasting is a kid who has never been off Harrow and is terrified of being left behind, which is exactly why he keeps climbing into crates.
- **Battle style:** Pulls random gadgets out of random pockets: traps, buffs, repairs and the occasional small explosion; signature move **Patent Pending**, an untested contraption with a wild (and occasionally amazing) result.
- **Arc:** Mox starts as a stowaway chasing glory and grows into the engineer who keeps every walker, titan and ship running when it matters most. Along the way he finds out the crew was never going to leave him behind, and he stops bragging about what he'll build and starts building it.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *"Stand back, everybody, I've done this a hundred times! ...Once. I've watched somebody do it once."*
  - *"You can tell me to go home. I'm just gonna get in the next crate."*

### Vela Quist
- **Role:** Healer and support. A Signals Corps listening specialist assigned to jam the call, who has to listen to it to do her job, actually listens, and at the end of Act 1 quits in style mid-battle by handing over her resignation form, in triplicate, then switching sides on the spot.
- **Look:** Chibi chinchilla: a perfectly round puff of soft silver-gray fur with big round ears and a short fluffy tail, the smallest member of the crew by a lot. Pressed blue-gray Signals Corps uniform (she keeps it pressed even after she quits, with the Corps patch swapped for a hand-stitched lamp), giant headphones clamped over her round ears, and an antenna pin on the collar. Signature items: a clipboard nearly as big as she is and a rubber stamp she wields like a holy relic. Silhouette: a round puffball behind giant headphones, clipboard raised like a shield.
- **Personality:** Prim, exact, and speaks seven languages, none of them casually. Quirk: she still writes noise tickets out of pure habit, including for Otis's humming, Mox's explosions and her own heroic war cries. Tally is the only member of the crew who has never earned one, which Vela finds deeply suspicious, so she has started logging Tally's nods, shrugs and thumbs-ups as formal statements for the record. At her core she is someone who heard a stranger asking for help, and could not unhear it, and that was the end of her career.
- **Battle style:** Heals, shields and tidies up Relay chains so they don't drop; signature move **Approved in Triplicate**, three loud stamps that patch up the whole party.
- **Arc:** Vela begins certain that following the rules is the same thing as doing good, and breaks the biggest rule on the frontier because listening was the right thing to do. Her careful notes on the call become one of the crew's most important tools. (twist-related beats: see RESTRICTED section)
- **Sample lines:**
  - *"Under regulation 14-C, I'm required to warn you before I do this. Consider yourself warned. Twice. Once more for my records."*
  - *"They told me to listen so I'd know what to silence. I listened. Somebody needs help. That's the whole message."*
  - *"Let the record show Tally nodded. Let the record also show she was out the door before I finished the question. Amending the record."*

### Ruo Vantell
> ✅ APPROVED by Ross, 2026-10-05

- **Role:** Fast attacker and thief; Coldrunner captain and the crew's ship. Joins in Act 2 when the party needs a ride to the Rim, flying his ship, the *Low Profile* (the loudest ship ever to fly cold).
- **Look:** Chibi raccoon with a dramatic striped tail and a mask that looks like he was born for this job. A long dark Coldrunner coat with the running-light patches painted over, a scarf he flips over his shoulder at every opportunity, and fingerless gloves. Signature items: a shuttered lantern on his belt (the Coldrunner symbol) and twin flare pistols. Silhouette: long coat, big tail, a lantern swinging behind him.
- **Personality:** Runs blockades, flirts with danger, and has a reputation even worse than his manners. Quirk: true to Coldrunner tradition, he scores everything out loud for style, from getaways to the crew's breakfast to his own entrances (always a ten). His big brother flew rimward to answer the call eleven years ago, and Ruo keeps a lamp lit in his cockpit that he swears is just for reading.
- **Battle style:** Quick strikes, item steals and showy finishers that cash in long Relay chains; signature move **Ten Out of Ten**, a flare-pistol volley that gets more dazzling the longer the chain before it.
- **Arc:** Ruo signs on claiming it's strictly for the fee, and spends Act 2 slowly admitting he was always going to the Rim anyway. The smuggler who has spent his whole life finding ways around things finally decides to go straight through. (twist-related beats: see RESTRICTED section)
- **Sample lines:**
  - *"That was a six. Sloppy landing, no flair. Watch me, I'll show you a ten."*
  - *"I've been flying the long way around the Rim for eleven years. Turns out I was just waiting for somebody stubborn enough to go straight."*
