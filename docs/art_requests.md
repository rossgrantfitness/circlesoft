**Vertical slice art list (42 assets: 41 approved 2026-10-06, plus the train-car kit, row 58, approved with the structure pass 2026-10-08).** The look: PSX style, Mega Man Legends, Tail Concerto, MGS1, FF7, **in the grim, slum-cyberpunk look Ross approved on 2026-10-08** (desaturated, darker, grimy; characters and enemies a little brighter than the world, with an edge light). All poly and texture numbers below are **starting budgets; final numbers set by the style guide**.

**Updated 2026-10-08:** rows 17, 19, 20, 21, 37, 47 and 50 follow the approved tone and structure passes (slum Harrow, the Spillway, the jammer works, the train inspectors, spray-paint stencil ratings); rows 56 (grim dressing kit) and 57 (grim Red) are now approved, not conditional; row 58 (train-car kit) is new.

**Priority follows the build order:** P1 = party and weapons (battle, M2; Red first) · P2 = Harrow Landing: portraits, townsfolk, town sets (M3) · P3 = the ore train, the Spillway and the jammer works: sets, enemies, props (M4) · P4 = the boss (M5) · P5 = UI (M7).

**Portraits: 24 images in 6 sets** (Red, Otis, Mox × 5 expressions = 15; Kasp + 2 key NPCs × 3 = 9). Crowd NPCs get none.

**Model contract (2026-10-06):** if a character model is outsourced, it must follow the "Model contract" in docs/style_guide.md: six clips named `idle`, `walk`, `run`, `jump`, `fall`, `land` (15 fps stepped), facing +Z in Godot (-Y in Blender), 1 unit = 1 m with Red 1.0 tall, origin at the feet, body at most 900 tris, 128x128 body texture plus 128x64 face sheet. The placeholder shiba Red (game/art/placeholder/characters/red/red_shiba.glb) is the working example, and Red's look is locked to prototype F (red_proto_f.glb). Gesture clips are on hold.

**Shared notes:** 3D models as .glb, textures as .png, nearest-neighbor, low color count (per the style guide). **Characters are animals in the approved grim look (2026-10-08):** taller and leaner than the old chibi, smaller heads, matte dusty fur with no gloss spots, faded, scuffed and stained clothes (see the story bible's Look lines and the style guide's Character proportions). The **edge light** is a shader effect done in code: don't paint rim light into textures. Paint characters a notch lighter in value than the environments they stand in ("brighter, not too bright"), and keep every texture dirty and muted before the grim screen grade. Weapons are separate props held in the hand, so models need a hand attach point. Animation lists come from the Technical Artist with the tech plan. Final art goes under game/art/final/ (exact folders confirmed by the tech plan); agents' placeholders stay in game/art/placeholder/.
