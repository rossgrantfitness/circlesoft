> Status: **APPROVED by Ross, 2026-10-06** ("D1 a d2 yes"): Relay Watch Zero, plus all toughen picks and the HNS prefix.
> Ross's brief: "change the name of the tenders, remember this is scifi fantasy so some of these names need to be like fucking bravo company or something"

---

## Decisions needed

DECISION NEEDED: What do we call the Tenders?
Option A: **Relay Watch Zero** — pros: punchy numbered unit, great callsign, built-in joke / cons: "Zero" is a common word in sci-fi
Option B: **Iron Bell Battery** — pros: turns their bells into artillery, very loud / cons: leans on one gag (the bells)
Option C: **First Mast Company** — pros: classic "X Company" ring, claims seniority / cons: the plainest of the three
Recommendation: **A, Relay Watch Zero.** It hits hardest, gives them a callsign Ross can hear grunts yelling, and the "Zero" joke plays off the Navy's forgotten helmet numbers.

DECISION NEEDED: Toughen the four soft group names in section 2 (Landing Corps, Salvage Works, the Yards, plus an optional ship prefix)?
Option A: Take the four picks below — pros: setting sounds like a war, jokes kept / cons: some find-and-replace in approved text
Option B: Tenders only, leave the rest — pros: smallest change / cons: Landing Corps and Salvage Works still read soft next to the new name
Recommendation: **A.** Ross asked for "some of these names", so change the group names that sound like a company, not the ship names that carry jokes.

## 1. The Tenders (must rename)

Who they are: cranky, fearless old-timers who guard the relay masts, ring their sleeve bells in protest, and keep the oldest records of the call.

| | Name | Callsign / nickname | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A (pick)** | **Relay Watch Zero** | "Zero." Members are "Zeroes." Roll call: "Watch Zero, sound off!" and every sleeve bell rings at once. | The Hegemony numbers everything starting at one; these folks were on the masts before anybody started counting. The Navy grunts forget their numbers; the Zeroes are proud of theirs. Quiet second-playthrough nod (see restricted section). |
| B | **Iron Bell Battery** | "Clang." "Clang's dug in at the mast." | A battery is artillery, and their artillery is noise. Fits the frontier record for noise tickets. |
| C | **First Mast Company** | "Old First." "Nobody outranks Old First." | They have been on the job longest and won't let anyone forget it. Fits the oldest records of the call. |

All three avoid "keep", so nothing clashes with the Keeper. "Caretakers of the relay masts" can stay as the description line.

Sample, Option A: Kasp: *"Every one of those bells is a noise violation, grandma."* Old Zero: *"Son, Watch Zero has been writing you up since before your empire was born."*

## 3. Tone balance

Groups get hard military names so the setting sounds like a war. Characters, ships and gags stay goofy, so the joke is a crew this rowdy flying under names this tough.
