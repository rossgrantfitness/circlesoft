# Image prompts for the slice art (text → image → Meshy 3D)

> For Ross, 2026-10-09. Paste a prompt into your image AI (e.g. Gemini), then give the image to Meshy. Same order as docs/slice/meshy_art_list.md. Edit freely: these are starting points, and the designs are yours.

## Tips that help Meshy

- **One object per image**, whole thing in frame, centred, nothing overlapping it, no text or logos.
- **Characters: A-pose** (arms down and out at about 45°), standing straight, feet apart. Meshy builds cleaner limbs from an A-pose. If a 3/4 view gives you a lumpy back, regenerate the same prompt as a **front view**.
- **Flat, even lighting**: no dramatic light, no rim light (the game adds its own edge light).
- **Breakable parts get their own image** (legs, relay box, dish, plates, core, clipboard, shield, drone).
- **Police machines: no cyan** (in our game cyan means "Red hacked this").
- **Robots stay original**: no face on the chest, no drill, no sunglasses, no spirals.

The shared opening, used in every prompt below:

> *create an image of a PS2 game asset model on a white background with no shadow, in a cyberpunk world, in a 3/4 view, stylized low-poly early-2000s 3D game style, clean readable shapes, painted textures, flat even lighting, single object, full body in frame,*
