## Two quick questions

DECISION NEEDED: How do you send the parts that break off or move (Hushmaster legs, relay boxes and dish; the junk mech's plates and core)?
Option A: **Everything as one Meshy model; we cut the parts off.** — Pros: least work for you. / Cons: Meshy fuses parts together, so cuts leave holes and jagged edges, and the breaks you watch most look messy.
Option B: **The body as one model, plus each breakable part as its own small model** (one leg we copy 8 times, one relay box copied 4 times, one dish, 2 or 3 plates we copy and mirror, one core). — Pros: clean breaks; about six extra small models, most of them reused. / Cons: a few more Meshy runs, and the style has to match across them.
Option C: **Body only; the breakable parts stay our blocks.** — Pros: fastest. / Cons: the parts you hit most look unfinished.
Recommendation: **B.** About six small models buys clean leg drops and plates tearing off, the moments the fight is built around.

DECISION NEEDED: What is Vela now that Red is a bunny? (Only needed when you reach the town people.)
Option A: **Keep her a bunny,** with upright ears to Red's lop ears. — Pros: no change. / Cons: two bunny girls blur "the hacker bunny" as Red's thing.
Option B: **A fennec fox:** huge ears for the radio listener who switched sides. — Pros: her ears tell you her job; a clearly different shape from Red. / Cons: a new design.
Option C: **Your pick.**
**Ross, 2026-10-09: B, a fennec fox** ("DONT WORRY ABOUT THIS FOR NOW BYT B I GUESS"). Not needed until the town people.

## Making them in Meshy

- **One model per file is fine.** Breakable or moving parts as their own files (question 1).
- **Characters and anything with arms and legs** (Kasp, the heavy, the town people, the junk mech): **A-pose, unrigged.** We rig them and put the free animations on.
- **Meshy's PBR textures are fine.** Swap in your own textures whenever you want.
- **The numbers are guides.** Set Meshy's polycount near them; going over is fine. Your Red at 1,614 triangles is the right amount of detail.
- **Don't paint rim light:** the edge light is done in code. Make characters a touch brighter than the place they stand in.
- **Keep cyan off the Signals machines:** cyan means "Red hacked this".
- **Robots are ours:** no Gurren Lagann look (no face on the chest, no drill, no sunglasses, no spirals), and the junk mech has no face at all. Docking is the one thing we borrow.
- Send files however is easiest; we'll put them in the right folders.

**Our working assumption:** town people are "cute people, grim skyline", as in the pitch you approved (warm, friendly faces; patched clothes; a grim city behind). If you want them cuter or grimmer, tell us.

**Still stand-ins until you say otherwise:** all animations (the free Quaternius set), the loader and the colossus (already on your list from the robot test), effects, sounds and music.
