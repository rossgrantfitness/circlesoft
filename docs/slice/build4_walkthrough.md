# Build 4 walkthrough (slice graybox)

> For Ross, 2026-10-10. Step by step, start to end. About 30 to 40 minutes.

## Buttons you need
- **Attack / talk / use:** J or left click. Near a person or a machine with a prompt, the attack button talks or uses.
- **Hack:** K or right click fires the chosen hack. **Pick the hack with keys 1 to 4** (or the mouse wheel): 1 Zap, 2 EMP, 3 Overclock, 4 Reboot (heal, costs the whole battery). The battery refills when your sword hits things.
- **Lock on:** Tab. **Dash:** Shift (5 charges). **Jump:** Space. **Pause, items and healing:** Esc.

## 1. Hideout (start)
Vela greets you over the radio. Use the desk computer (Attack) to save. Leave by the door into the square.

## 2. Tarp Square (the town)
- Go to **Dispatch**, the first door on the back wall next to your hideout. Use the job board and take the **"Yard 9"** job. You get the **Courier Pass**.
- Optional: the **Corner Shop** (healing items) and the **Forge** (swords) are on the same wall.
- Go through the **arch on the north side** to **Gate 4**. Walk up to the guard at the barrier; he checks your pass and lifts it. Walk through.

## 3. J1 Yard Gate
Walk east down the lane. Two wolves by the burning barrel: lock on (Tab) and fight. Then look up at the glowing **fuse box on a pole**: press **1** (Zap), then **K**. The door on the east side opens.

## 4. J2 Scrap Canyon
- **The chute:** a wall turret fires down the lane. Run past it or Zap it.
- **The Pit Stop** (the big yard): 3 wolves, then a Brute. Run up the **scrap ramp on the north side** to the turret and press **3** (Overclock), then **K**: it shoots the enemies for you for 10 seconds.
- **The gate:** walk up the ramp at the far end and use the **terminal** (Attack). The gate opens.

## 5. J3 Crane Yard
- A pit splits the yard. **Fight** the wolves and Brute in the west half.
- Find the **crane control box** at the foot of the crane, on the west side by the pit. Press **3** (Overclock), then **K**. The crane picks up the girder and lays it across the pit as a bridge (about 8 seconds). If it drops it, refill your battery by hitting enemies or drones and try again.
- Drones keep coming from a machine in the northwest corner: **EMP** (2) stops them, or Zap the box on its roof to stop them for good.
- Cross the bridge. **Save** at the terminal in the shed on the east side (the last save on foot). Leave by the east door.

## 6. J4 Wreck Row
- Walk past the giant robot parts into **the Stand**. Shutters close behind you: 2 Brutes, 3 wolves, drones, 2 turrets. **Overclock the turrets** to help. There's a Ration Bar on the ground, and more healing under Esc → Items.
- When it's clear, go east to the **loader robot** in its cradle. **Use it (Attack) when the prompt shows.** It wakes up. **Walk into it** to climb in.
- In the loader, **attack the scrap wall on the east side** to smash through.

## 7. J5 Smash Run (in the loader)
Keep heading **east**, smashing walls, cars and containers. At the far end, smash the **big gate** (it takes a few hits).

## 8. Kasp's arena, phase 1: the Hushmaster
- Red climbs out of the loader. **Save** at the terminal in the shed just left of the gate. **Remember where the loader is parked (by the gate): you'll need it again.**
- Walk **up the ramp** to the plateau. The fight starts.
- **Zap the four leg relays** (boxes on the legs). The **LEGS** counter under the boss bar shows your progress.
- **Jump** the shock ring when a leg lifts and slams. **Dash** through the red line when the dish sweeps a beam.
- **EMP** the drones it drops.
- When the screen says **JAMMING: HIT THE DISH!**, Zap or hit the dish fast, or your hacks lock for 5 seconds.
- All four legs down: it falls. Press **K** for **Jack in** (a big hit).

## 9. The turn (this is where it's easy to get lost)
Kasp escapes, and the yard's scrap builds a giant mech, **the Heap**. **Run back down the ramp, south, to the gate where you left the loader, and walk into it.** From there it's automatic: the loader drives to the colossus and docks.

## 10. Phase 2: the colossus against the Heap
- Watch the **floor markings** and the Heap's **lamps (orange, then red)**: step or dash out of the marked area.
- After an attack, one of its **plates lights up pale blue**: hit that one. Break all four plates, then the **core** in its chest.
- Smash **containers** for repair cells (healing).

## 11. Ending
Kasp is beaten. Walk home through Gate 4 into the square.
