# Pitch Round 1 (rejected 2026-10-05)

> Kept for the record. Ross passed on all three. See round_2.md for the new direction.

PITCH A: NINTH LIFE (alt: LANTERN ROAD)
Logline: A cat who carries the souls of the dead is told she's on her ninth and final life. She sets out across the stars to relight the dying sun that sends every soul to its next life.
World: A ring of moons circles a dying star, the Lantern; every soul flies to it to be reborn. Animal-folk samurai clans, lacquered-wood starships, shrine gates that fold space. Magic is prayer-script on paper charms. The Lantern is guttering.
Cast: Kesa (calico cat, soul courier, deadpan); Brother Hachibei (tanuki, fake monk, secretly devout); Rokuro (grey wolf, the man rebirth refuses, unkillable); Raiha (crow, sky-pirate engineer, atheist).
Battle hook: Dying is a move. A fallen character returns next turn as the next of four "lives" on their mask wheel: full HP, a different job, plus a parting move fired as they fall. Some bosses beatable only in the right life. Party wipe sends you to the last shrine, but the world moves forward and remembers (shopkeepers comment, bosses taunt you).
Twist (kind): Someone lied to Kesa about how many lives she's had and whose they were; every death counts, including game overs.
Slice: Town Lanternfall (stepped fishing town on a moon, funeral boats sail skyward); dungeon Ossuary Locks (flooded lantern canal beneath); boss the Toll-Keeper (giant lamprey ferryman demanding one death to pass, teaches dying-as-a-move).
Arc: Act 1 underworld beneath the moon, Act 2 the ring of worlds, Act 3 into the star and beyond. Kesa reborn each act with a new coat pattern (texture swap, not a new model).

PITCH B: DOWN TO THE STARS (alt: SHELLBREAK)
Logline: Tunnel kids discover their world is a hollow shell with a fake sun at the center, and the real stars lie beneath their own feet.
World: Life on the inside of a giant sphere around a central sun. The Sun Court rules the sunlit lands; tunnel-folk live in the crust below.
Cast: Mitsu (flying squirrel, tunnel courier, huge promises); Banzo (pangolin, gentle-giant mine foreman, afraid of open sky); Captain Sazanami (snow leopard, by-the-book Sun Court knight, bad poetry); Nishiki (koi spirit, sleepy ancient pilot of a fish-shaped starship).
Battle hook: Vows. Swear a vow before a turn ("no damage this round", "finish in three turns"); keep it to build Nerve for team attacks, break it for a funny shame penalty. At set moments the koi ship fights with the same commands at bigger scale; the ship grows across acts (koi → winged carp → dragon).
Twist (kind): The shell isn't what it looks like, and breaking out is what something out there has been waiting for.
Slice: Town Burrowdeep (root-cavern, glowing moss); koi ship crashes through the roof with Sazanami aboard; dungeon the flooded Sump (race Sun Court soldiers to the wreck); boss Crust Warden (stone centipede). Slice ends with the ship rising into sunlight.
Arc: Act 1 break up into the sunlit inside; Act 2 take the inner world, sun revealed as a lamp; Act 3 dive down through home into the real stars.

PITCH C: HUNGRY GHOSTS (alt: WHEELWORKS)
Logline: A rat thief reborn poor forty times robs the temple-company that decides what everyone is reborn as.
World: Rain-soaked space-station city orbiting a gas giant. Rebirth is an industry run by the Wheelworks temple; karma score decides your next body. Tigers and cranes in the spires, rats and oxen at the bottom. Karma is also money.
Cast: Kagero (rat thief, sardonic); Madame Bengara (tiger, defected aristocrat, parasol blade); Ushimaru (ox bodyguard, speaks in proverbs); Yomi (fruit bat, child medium hearing the dead in the network).
Battle hook: Borrow from your next life. Push past limits by taking a karma loan; finish fast or a Hungry Ghost debt collector appears mid-battle or hunts you into the next fight. Karma is also shop money.
Twist (kind): The ladder everyone is climbing runs the opposite way from what they've been told.
Slice: Town Gutter Tier (noodle-stall market, capsule shrines); dungeon a sneak-in heist on a Wheelworks rebirth clinic; boss the Abbot-Auditor (peacock enforcer who punishes karma debt).
Arc: undercity → spires → orbital temple → the Wheel and the god who runs it.

Slice scope for all: one town, one dungeon, one boss, three party members, full battle system, ~30 min story. PSX low-poly look like Mega Man Legends / Fear Effect. Ross wants long cutscenes. One artist (Ross), small AI studio, Godot 4.
