## Ross's brief (2026-10-05)

What Ross wants, in his words and ours:

- **The climb:** on foot → big robot → giant robot → giant spaceship → intergalactic god. Every stage of scale is a step up the ladder, all the way to fighting (and becoming) a god.
- **Setting:** sci-fi first, with fantasy and animal anime elements mixed in.
- **Characters:** humanoids first and foremost. Animal traits are an anime flavor (ears, tails, markings, instincts), not talking animals, and not gimmicks like "nine lives."
- **Party-based RPG:** a main character with a real supporting cast who travel and fight together.
- **Still true from round 1:** death, rebirth and "try, die, try again" as a theme; long cutscenes; a look like Mega Man Legends or Fear Effect; loves FF7, MGS1, Fallout 3, Chrono Trigger/Cross, FF8, FF9, FFX, DQ7.
- **What didn't work in round 1:** characters that were simply animals, and mechanics built on animal gimmicks. Not enough robots and spaceships.

## Studio guardrails
- Original: the escalating-scale climb is welcome, but no copying Gurren Lagann's signature beats (no drill motif, no spiral-energy equivalent, no copied opening or story beats), and nothing lifted from any other work.
- The vertical slice (one town, one dungeon, one boss, three party members, full battle system, ~30 min story) must come from each pitch's opening, so it starts at the "on foot" stage, with a taste of the robot to come.
