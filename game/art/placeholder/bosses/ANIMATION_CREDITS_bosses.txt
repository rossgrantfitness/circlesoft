They were retargeted offline (Red's bone names kept) by scripts/tools/retarget_ual.py from data/animation/retarget_kasp.json and
retarget_junk_mech.json (task VS-25). No clip was authored by hand or by code: every clip is an existing Quaternius clip, sliced,
sped up or slowed (the junk mech's), joined, held at its last pose, or played mirrored (Scrap Swing, left arm = Sword_Regular_A
reflected). The source of each clip is in data/animation/kasp_clip_keys.json and junk_mech_clip_keys.json ("source").
Source clips used: Kasp: Idle_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop, Idle_Talking_Loop, Yes, Hit_Chest, Hit_Knockback, LayToIdle, Death01.
Junk mech: Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Sword_Regular_A, Sword_Regular_A_Rec, Sword_Heavy_Combo, OverhandThrow, Hit_Chest, Zombie_Scratch, Death01.
The meshes are the Technical Artist's placeholder blockouts (scripts/tools/make_bosses.py, task VS-24); the original kasp.glb and
junk_mech.glb beside them are untouched.
