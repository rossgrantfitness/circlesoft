They were retargeted onto the Cyberwolf Sentinel offline (bone names kept) by scripts/tools/retarget_ual.py from the settings in
data/animation/retarget_*.json. The mesh, skeleton and textures are the rigged copy beside this file; the clips are placeholders
(Ross, 2026-10-08: everything is placeholder for now). No animation in this file was authored by hand or by code (the stagger, launched and block_hold clips hold a pose or ping-pong a slice of a library clip; retreat and dodge_back are library clips played backwards; strafe_l and strafe_r are the walk with the hips and legs turned).
